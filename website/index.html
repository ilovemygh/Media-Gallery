A library that stays yours

Find it. Play it.
Move it when you need.

01

Browse without the hunt

Bring photos, videos, and music into one searchable library. Sort by date, place, or name, with your originals left where they are.

02

Pair the devices you own

Connect your PC and phone with a QR code or PIN. Browse remote thumbnails and transfer selected files directly between paired devices.

03

Your files stay with you

Your library and media files stay on your devices by default. Transfers are device to device, with no media file uploads to a cloud library.

From install to transfer

Three steps.
Your own devices.

No account needed to start browsing your local library.

  1. 01

    Install Media Gallery

    Choose the installer for your computer or the APK for Android.

  2. 02

    Open Devices

    Show a QR code or six-digit PIN from the device you want to pair.

  3. 03

    Browse and transfer

    Explore your libraries and send selected files directly between devices.
